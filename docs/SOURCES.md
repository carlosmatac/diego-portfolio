# Sources register

Every biographical claim on the site, where it comes from and how solid it is. The same ids live in
`src/data/sources.ts`; `src/data/facts.ts` attaches them to each entry. Wording is in `src/i18n/en.ts`.

How the research was done: the LinkedIn profile (`/in/diegopradosjodar/`) answers HTTP 999 to scripts, so its
content was read from the public search snippets of the profile and from Diego's own public LinkedIn posts, then
cross-checked against official pages. Nothing was taken from people with similar names (a "Diego Prados" with an
Economics degree from Loughborough/UC3M is a different person and was discarded).

Confidence: **A** official or institutional page; **B** Diego's own public post or author bio; **C** LinkedIn profile
snippet only (to be confirmed by Diego); **D** Diego's own word or his private CV (`Diego_Prados_Jodar_CV.pdf`, gitignored), not publicly checkable.
