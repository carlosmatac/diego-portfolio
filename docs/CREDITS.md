# Credits

## Typefaces (SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted through Fontsource)
- Archivo (variable, weight and width axes): headlines, navigation and greetings. Chosen as a legal, widely available grotesque with the weight and presence of the two reference sites' type (MG12 at gravity-design.de and PP Neue Montreal at perappelgren.de, both commercial or private).
- Source Serif 4 (variable): reading text.
- Noto Sans JP, hiragana subset only (`src/assets/fonts/`): the Japanese greeting.
